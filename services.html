Our services

Support for every step of the job journey

Career planning, job development, placement, coaching and benefits planning for individuals with IDD, ages 16 and up.

What we do

Services that support the whole journey

From figuring out what kind of work you'd enjoy to staying successful once you're hired, we're with you along the way.

Career Planning

We get to know you: your goals, availability, transportation, likes and dislikes, sensitivities, and any barriers to employment, plus how to overcome them.

Job Development

Creating or updating your resume, searching for jobs that match your wants and needs, applying, following up on applications, and preparing for interviews.

Job Placement

We advocate for you to get interviews and offers, make sure the job is a good fit before you accept, and talk with managers about any accommodations you'll need on day one.

Job Coaching

On-the-job support to make sure you're fully trained, completing tasks to standard, and building natural support connections with coworkers.

Benefits Planning

Help getting and keeping benefits such as SSDI and food assistance, and understanding exactly how working will affect them, from certified Work Incentive Practitioners.

Ongoing Support

We check in with you and your employer to make sure everyone is satisfied, and provide accommodations, coaching, or extra training if anything needs attention.

How we work

Support that meets you where you are

  1. 1

    Getting to know you

    We learn about your goals, availability, transportation, what you like and don't like, sensitivities, and anything that might get in the way.

  2. 2

    Finding the right fit

    We search for jobs that match your wants and needs, help you apply, and advocate for you to get interviews.

  3. 3

    Starting strong

    Before you accept, we make sure the job is a good fit, and we work with your manager on any accommodations you need.

  4. 4

    Staying successful

    Job coaching and ongoing check-ins with you and your employer help you build a lasting career.
